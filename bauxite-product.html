What is Bauxite?

Bauxite is the world's primary source of aluminum, a sedimentary rock with high aluminum content. Our bauxite is sourced from the rich deposits of Guinea Conakry, which contain some of the highest quality ore globally with superior alumina content and minimal impurities.

Guinea holds the world's largest bauxite reserves, and our operations focus on sustainable extraction methods that minimize environmental impact while maximizing resource efficiency.

Bauxite Applications

Versatile mineral with diverse industrial uses across multiple sectors

Aluminum Production

Primary raw material for alumina refineries and aluminum smelters worldwide through the Bayer process and Hall-Héroult process.

Refractory Products

Used in manufacturing refractory bricks and materials for high-temperature applications in steel, glass, and cement industries.

Cement Manufacturing

Acts as a source of alumina in specialty cements and as a raw material modifier in Portland cement production.

Chemical Applications

Source material for aluminum chemicals, water treatment compounds, and catalysts for various industrial processes.
